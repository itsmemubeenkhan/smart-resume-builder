How to Delete Your Data

Smart Resume Builder stores your resume data locally on your device. You have full control over your data.

Option 1: Delete from within the app

  1. Open Smart Resume Builder
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap "Delete All Data"
  4. Confirm deletion

Option 2: Clear app data (Android)

  1. Go to Android Settings → Apps → Smart Resume Builder
  2. Tap "Storage"
  3. Tap "Clear Data"

Option 3: Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Smart Resume Builder will delete all locally stored data from your device.
